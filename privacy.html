Privacy Policy for rclone-nas

Last updated: October 13, 2026

rclone-nas is a personal, non-commercial application used to connect the owner's Synology NAS to Google Drive through rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application requests access to Google Drive to list, read, upload, modify, and delete files as directed by the owner.

Data Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's NAS configuration. The application does not operate a separate data collection server and does not sell user data.

Revoking Access

Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For inquiries, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
